## WHAT'S NEW

### Track selector

The **TRACKS** tab now carries a pill selector. Right now it holds one track ("Ain't No Trick"); the structure supports adding a handful of contrasting tracks so the demo can show how Apple Music Understanding reads different kinds of music. Click a pill to switch tracks — the analysis, audio, and info all follow.

### Cleaner track panel

Cover art and track details now share one bordered panel, with the shader in a matching panel beside it. A plain copyright line sits with the track info. The AMU/OS attribution moved into the ANALYSIS header where it belongs.

### It just plays

Served over the web, Sozo auto-loads the track's analysis and audio and plays against the timeline with no steps for the visitor — click the link, hit play, watch the analysis track the music.

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Open the page (or the published link).
2. If it doesn't auto-play, use **load audio** / **load .json**.
3. Press **play** (or spacebar). Watch the STRUCTURE timeline and the reacting visual.
4. Click any section / segment / phrase block to lock and inspect it.
5. Open the **JSON** tab to see the raw Music Understanding analysis; **SUPPORT** for how it works; **ABOUT** for background.
